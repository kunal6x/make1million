A few useful details.

Do I need a logo and professional photos?

A logo is optional; a clean text treatment works too. Supply photos you own or have permission to use. We can build a simple text-led layout if you don’t have suitable photos.

Can I use my existing booking tool?

Yes. We link to your existing booking page, phone number, or email. A new scheduling system, payment backend, or customer database is outside this scope.

What happens after delivery?

You receive source files and deployment instructions. You own your business’s page and choose where to host it. Hosting, domain purchases, ongoing content changes, and maintenance aren’t included in the $350 fee.

When does the two-day target start?

After we accept the project, receive complete assets, and confirm payment. Delivery means your first review version; the included revision follows your consolidated feedback. Timing is confirmed before payment, and weekends or holidays may affect the schedule.
