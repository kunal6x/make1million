Scope & privacy

DetailPage Studio is a working service brand. The seller's legal identity, a monitored contact address, and payment arrangements must be supplied in a written project agreement before any paid engagement.

The $350 USD project

One mobile-ready page for one detailing business: services, supplied pricing, service area, supplied photos and branding, and a call or existing booking link. Includes one consolidated revision and source files. A private concept and written scope agreement come before payment. The delivery target is two business days after scope acceptance, payment, and receipt of all required materials.

Domain registration, hosting, paid software, custom booking systems, advertising, legal copy, and ongoing maintenance are outside the price. You retain control of your accounts and buy any agreed external services directly. No sales, traffic, search ranking, or revenue outcome is promised. The project agreement must set the actual delivery date, acceptance process, and cancellation/refund terms before an invoice is sent.

The demonstration

Northline Mobile Detailing is fictional. Its services, prices, location, and contact actions are illustrative. The demonstration accepts no bookings or payments.

Project inquiries

When inquiry collection is enabled, the form collects your name, business email, business name, optional website, and project description to assess and respond to your request. Do not send passwords, payment details, or sensitive personal information. Submitting an inquiry does not create a contract or reserve delivery.

Inquiries are accessible to the service operator and are not published or sold. Contact the business email displayed on the main page to request access or deletion. No analytics or advertising trackers are included. If no business contact is displayed, the form remains disabled and no inquiry is accepted.
