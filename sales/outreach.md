# Personal outreach drafts

These are templates, not sent messages. Research is not permission to contact someone. External sending requires an authenticated sender, a real sender identity, and explicit sending authorization. Keep messages individual and relevant; respect channel rules, opt-outs, and applicable commercial-message requirements. If using US commercial email, configure accurate sender information, a valid postal address, and a working opt-out process before sending.

Use only publicly listed business contact routes intended for business inquiries. Do not infer private addresses, scrape private accounts, evade platform limits, or send through a customer-only booking form. Where no appropriate route is listed, mark the prospect unavailable and move on.

## Required prospect checks

Before using a draft, verify the business is active, serves a US location, offers mobile detailing, appears independent, and has a specific website opportunity supported by the current public page. Capture the source URL, date checked, public contact route, and one concrete observation. If a source cannot be verified, label it unverified and do not fill gaps with guesses.

Good observation: “On the mobile services page, the call link appears below the full gallery.” Unsupported observation: “Your website costs you ten customers every week.” A directory listing without an obvious website is not proof that no website exists.

## First introduction

Subject: A mobile service-page idea for [Business]

Hi [publicly verified name, or business team],

I looked at [specific public page] and noticed [one accurate, relevant observation]. I build simple mobile pages for independent detailers: services, service area, and a clear call or existing booking link in one place.

The fixed price is $350, including one revision and source files. I can show you a small private concept first. The complete preview is due two business days after we agree the scope and receive the materials and payment.

Would a concept for [Business] be useful?

[Real sender name and business contact]
[Required postal address, if sent by commercial email]
If this is not useful, reply “no thanks” and I will not follow up.

## One follow-up, at least five business days later

Subject: Re: A mobile service-page idea for [Business]

Hi [name/team],

One follow-up on the $350 service page for [Business]. The specific idea is [one concise improvement tied to the verified observation]. I can share a private concept if that is useful. Otherwise, no action is needed and I will close this out.

[Real sender identity and required contact/address details]
Reply “no thanks” to opt out.

After this message, stop unless the prospect responds. Stop immediately for an opt-out, complaint, wrong recipient, or unsuitable channel.

Never paste a placeholder link into an outbound message. Never mark an invoice paid from a screenshot alone; verify it in the provider account.

## Tracking

Create a `pipeline.csv` row only for a real verified prospect. Keep drafted, sent, replied, paid, and settled states distinct. A researched email address is not a sent message; a positive response is not revenue. Store only necessary business contact information and do not commit sensitive correspondence or payment details.
