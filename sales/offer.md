# A clear booking page for an independent mobile detailer

**Price: $350 USD once.** A custom single-page website that makes services, service area, and the next step easy to find on a phone. This is a new service with no claimed client results or testimonials.

## Included

- A responsive page with the business name, client-approved description, up to six service entries, service area, hours, and a clear call button or link to an existing booking service.
- Client-owned photos, logo, prices, and factual claims supplied or approved by the client. Text-only presentation is available if photos are unavailable.
- Page title and description, accessible headings, readable contrast, and basic keyboard checks.
- A private preview, one consolidated revision round within the agreed scope, and source files with a short handoff guide.
- Help publishing to a client-owned static hosting account, if access is available. The client pays any domain or hosting costs directly.

No custom booking system, payments system, customer database, ongoing maintenance, advertising, guaranteed traffic, search ranking, or sales result is included. Additions require a separate agreement; do not silently expand scope or charge extra.

## Order sequence

1. Verify a suitable business and prepare a small, private, clearly labeled concept using factual public information. Do not publish it as the business's official website or imply a client relationship. Use placeholders instead of copying unlicensed photographs or branding.
2. Agree the scope, delivery method, approved text/assets, and exact call or booking destination in writing. The client reviews the concept before deciding.
3. Send a $350 invoice through the verified business payment account. Payment is due before production work begins. Never collect card details directly.
4. Deliver the first complete private preview within **two business days after accepted scope, complete assets, and confirmed payment**. Business days are Monday–Friday, excluding holidays agreed in the order. Put the actual delivery date in the order.
5. Make the single agreed revision round, obtain written acceptance, and hand over source files. Publish only with explicit client approval and appropriate access.

If the seller cannot deliver the agreed scope, offer a corrected deadline the client accepts or a refund through the original payment provider. Handle cancellation and refund terms before invoicing; do not invent a blanket “no refunds” policy.
