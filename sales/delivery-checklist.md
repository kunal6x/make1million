# $350 project delivery checklist

## Before accepting the order

- [ ] Real client contact and authority to approve the website are confirmed.
- [ ] Written scope includes sections, up to six service entries, service area, call/booking destination, one revision, source handoff, and any publication assistance.
- [ ] Exact price, applicable tax if any, cancellation/refund terms, and an actual first-preview date are accepted.
- [ ] Client has supplied or approved all copy, factual claims, prices, hours, and contact details.
- [ ] Client has rights to supplied images and logos. No copied competitor photos, invented awards, or invented testimonials are used.
- [ ] Payment is confirmed in the verified provider account. A customer's payment confirmation is separate from payout settlement.
- [ ] Hosting remains client-owned; any domain/hosting purchase is made and paid directly by the client. Account access is shared through supported invitations, never passwords in chat.

## Build and private preview

- [ ] Replace all example business names, links, phone numbers, images, prices, and placeholders with approved information.
- [ ] Build one static page; add no backend, tracking pixels, contact-data collection, or custom payment handling without a separate agreement.
- [ ] Check a narrow mobile viewport and desktop layout. Check text overflow, image sizing, navigation, keyboard focus, and readable contrast.
- [ ] Verify every call button uses the approved number and every booking link reaches the approved service.
- [ ] Check title, description, favicon if provided, headings, image descriptions, and absence of broken resources.
- [ ] Avoid oversized assets and unneeded dependencies. Run the project's documented local checks and browser smoke test; keep evidence concise.
- [ ] Share a private preview only with appropriate authorization. A `noindex` tag alone does not make a publicly reachable URL private.
- [ ] Make the proposal/draft status clear until the client approves publication. No official relationship is implied before agreement.

## Revision and acceptance

- [ ] Collect one consolidated revision list and identify anything outside the agreed scope before making new commitments.
- [ ] Complete in-scope corrections and recheck affected behavior. Fix defects in the agreed delivery without mislabeling them as an extra feature.
- [ ] Obtain written acceptance. Do not infer acceptance from silence.
- [ ] Remove proposal-only disclaimers from an approved client website where appropriate; retain truthful service information.

## Handoff and accounting

- [ ] Supply source files plus instructions for local preview and deployment, with no credentials or unrelated client data.
- [ ] Publish only with explicit client approval to their chosen account; verify HTTPS, the live URL, call/booking links, and mobile layout afterward.
- [ ] Record delivery and acceptance dates in `pipeline.csv`.
- [ ] Reconcile actual payment, provider fees, refunds, direct costs, payout date, and settlement evidence in `revenue.csv`.
- [ ] Mark settlement only after funds reach the seller's payout account. Do not count an issued invoice or pending balance as settled cash.
- [ ] Record remaining obligations, if any, and the agreed handoff/support boundary. Request a referral or testimonial only through an authorized channel and only after acceptance.

## Capacity

Allow approximately 4–6 working hours per ordinary project as an initial estimate; measure actual time and adjust. Keep at most two production builds active initially. The two-business-day promise starts only when scope, assets, and payment are complete, and the actual agreed date controls. Do not accept a deadline beyond available capacity.
